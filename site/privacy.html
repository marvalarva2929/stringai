1. Who we are

StringAI is a mobile application that analyzes musical-instrument practice (starting with violin) and provides coaching. The App is owned and operated by Joshua Vigel, an individual, based in the State of Texas, United States. We are the “data controller” for the information described here. You can reach us at joshvigel@gmail.com.

2. Children & age requirement

StringAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account or use the App. If you are between 13 and the age of majority in your jurisdiction, you may use StringAI only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact joshvigel@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly. We do not sell the personal information of minors, and we do not knowingly process it for advertising.

3. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Account information — your email address and a password (passwords are stored only in salted, hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see your plain-text password).
  • Profile & practice inputs — instrument, skill level, goals, and the title/composer of pieces you enter. If you upload a piece of sheet music (e.g., a PDF), we store that file so you can reuse it.
  • Support communications — messages you send us and the contact details in them.

b. Practice recordings (processed on your device)

When you record or upload a practice session, StringAI captures video and audio of your playing. This raw video and audio is analyzed on your device. We do not upload your raw practice video or audio to our servers. Recordings are stored locally on your device, and only a limited number of recent recordings are retained there (older ones are automatically deleted).

c. Analysis data (derived on device, stored in your account)

From the on-device analysis we generate non-media data such as: per-note pitch and timing values, tone and rhythm measurements, body/hand position keypoints (numeric coordinates — not images), metric scores, detected patterns, and the resulting written coaching. This derived data (typically a few kilobytes per session) is associated with your account so you can see your history and progress.

d. Subscription & purchase information

If you buy a subscription, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google through their in-app purchase systems, and managed for us by our subscription provider (RevenueCat). We receive your subscription status and a purchase/transaction identifier. We do not collect or store your credit-card or payment-card number — that is handled by the app store.

e. Device & usage information

  • Technical data — device type, operating-system version, app version, and a randomly generated app/user identifier, used to operate and troubleshoot the App.
  • Push notification token — if you enable notifications, a device token used to deliver practice reminders.
  • Diagnostics — basic crash and error information to keep the App working.

We do not currently use third-party advertising networks, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

4. Camera, microphone & photos

StringAI requests these device permissions only for their stated purpose:

  • Camera — to record your playing so posture and bow technique can be analyzed.
  • Microphone — to capture your sound so intonation, tone, and rhythm can be analyzed.
  • Photo library — only if you choose to upload an existing practice video.
  • Notifications — only if you opt in to practice reminders.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device settings; some features will stop working without them.

5. Artificial intelligence disclosure

StringAI uses artificial intelligence (AI) to help produce your coaching. We want to be transparent about this, consistent with U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidance on AI and honest advertising.

  • What is AI-generated. The written coaching, explanations, exercise suggestions, and any chat responses in the App are generated in whole or in part by an automated AI system (a third-party large language model). They are produced by software, not by a human music teacher reviewing your session.
  • What we send to the AI. To generate coaching, we send the AI provider a small, structured summary of your session — for example, which technical patterns were detected, your metric trends, and the piece’s style. We do not send your raw practice video or audio to the AI provider.
  • Our AI provider. AI coaching is generated using Anthropic’s Claude models, accessed through our secure server. Under Anthropic’s commercial API terms, inputs we send are not used to train their models. We do not sell your data to any AI company.
  • Accuracy & limitations. AI can be wrong. Its measurements and advice are estimates and may contain errors, may not fit your specific situation, and are not a substitute for a qualified music teacher or for professional, medical, or physical-therapy advice. Do not rely on StringAI for any decision where an error could cause injury or harm. See our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer, including guidance on avoiding physical strain or injury.
  • No consequential automated decisions. StringAI’s AI does not make legal, financial, employment, or similar decisions about you. It provides practice feedback only.
